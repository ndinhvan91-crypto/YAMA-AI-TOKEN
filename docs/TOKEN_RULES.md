
# YAMA TOKEN - CREDIT RULES v1.0

## 1. Overview
YAMA Credits are internal platform usage units.
They are not cryptocurrency or legal tender.

## 2. Initial Credits
- New verified account: 1,000 Credits.
- One-time welcome bonus.
- No cash withdrawal.
- No user-to-user transfers.

## 3. AI Service Pricing

| Service | Credits |
|---|---:|
| AI Chat | 10 |
| AI Translate | 5 |
| AI Writing | 8 |
| AI Code | 20 |
| AI Image | 100 |

These are initial test prices, not guaranteed commercial prices.

## 4. Credit Transactions

Allowed transaction types:
- signup_bonus
- purchase
- usage
- refund
- admin_adjustment

Every transaction must be recorded.

## 5. Security
- Users cannot modify their own balance.
- Backend controls all credit operations.
- Every request must have a unique request ID.
- No negative balances.
- Administrative adjustments must be audited.

## 6. Refund Policy
Failed AI operations may qualify for a credit refund.
Refunds must be authorized and recorded.

## 7. Payment Policy
Real payments are disabled during development.
Payment credits must only be issued after verified payment confirmation.

## 8. Privacy
User data must only be accessible according to authorized permissions.

## 9. Development Status
Version: 1.0
Mode: Internal Credits
Blockchain: Disabled
Real Payments: Disabled
